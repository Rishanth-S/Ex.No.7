# **Explanation:**

### **Prompt (Given):**

**“Design a personal productivity assistant that can help manage daily tasks, schedule reminders, suggest wellness tips, and answer general queries. The assistant should interact using natural language and be adaptable to the user's changing preferences over time.”**

This experiment focuses on applying prompt engineering to design a personal assistant that can interact naturally with users, handle multiple tasks, and adjust itself to user needs.
The assistant behaves as a conversational AI that organizes the user’s day and improves productivity through structured, well-designed prompts.

---

# **Procedure**

---

### **1. Define the Core Requirements of the Personal Productivity Assistant**

The assistant must be able to:
• Capture tasks and reminders
• Manage schedules and avoid conflicts
• Provide general information and meaningful suggestions
• Offer wellness and motivational guidance
• Interact naturally with users
• Learn or simulate user preferences over time

---

### **2. Identify and Construct Prompts for Each Task**

#### **A. Task Management Prompt**

*“Add this to my task list: Complete the report by 5 PM and remind me at 4 PM.”*

#### **B. Scheduling Prompt**

*“Schedule a meeting with the design team tomorrow at 11 AM and check for any conflicts.”*

#### **C. Query Prompt**

*“What is the easiest way to organize study notes for engineering subjects?”*

#### **D. Wellness Prompt**

*“Give me a daily wellness tip based on my routine: I study for long hours using a laptop.”*

#### **E. Preference-Based Prompt (Adaptive Pattern)**

*“Suggest productivity hacks based on the tasks I completed last week.”*

---

### **3. Simulate User Interaction Through a Simple Interface**

Using console/terminal or ChatGPT interface:

**User Input:** “Add a reminder to drink water every 2 hours.”
**Assistant Output:** “Reminder added! I’ll notify you every 2 hours to stay hydrated.”

**User Input:** “What should be my priority today?”
**Assistant Output:** “Based on your deadlines, completing the project presentation is the top priority.”

---

### **4. Collect Feedback from User and Adapt Responses**

Example:

**User Feedback:** “I prefer shorter reminders.”
**Adapted Response:** “Got it! I’ll keep reminders short and to the point.”

This simulates basic preference memory.

---

### **5. Integrate Basic Memory Simulation**

Example memory-based response:

**User Input:** “Give me a wellness tip.”
**Assistant Output:**
“Since you spend long hours at your system, take a 5-minute stretch break every hour.”

This shows simple “preference-learning behavior.”

# **Expected Output (LLM Example)**

### **Personal Productivity Assistant – Generated Features**

---

### **1. Daily Task Manager**

• Accepts natural-language task inputs.
• Maintains to-do lists with priorities and deadlines.
• Provides daily summaries of pending tasks.

### **2. Smart Scheduler**

• Creates events and reminders with contextual understanding.

### **3. Wellness Tips Generator**

### **4. General Query Assistant**

• Answers user questions across topics.
• Provides quick explanations, definitions, and recommendations.

### **5. Preference Adaptation (Simulated Memory)**

• Tracks previous instructions for improved personalization.
• Adjusts responses (brief, detailed, formal, casual).
• Maintains consistency with the user's workflow patterns.
